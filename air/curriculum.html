An ambition can give the learning a purpose

TENNIS BECOMES
A MATH YEAR.

A 36-week seventh-grade mathematics map for a fictional tennis player with a long-term ambition: play Division I tennis and reach Stanford.

The map connects a tennis season to statistics, budgeting, geometry and a scouting report. The interest gives the mathematics a reason to matter.

FROM THE PLAN
TO THE LESSONS.

A complete curriculum needs more than a sequence of topics. It needs the explanations, activities, support and evidence that make the sequence teachable.

  • A coherent course sequence and learning goals
  • Lessons, investigations and worked examples
  • Projects, assessment choices and answer support
  • Print materials, a course website or interactive tools as needed

A useful first conversation

TELL ME
ABOUT THEM.

What holds their attention? What are they hoping to do? What feels easy, difficult, or pointless right now? Include the subject, age or stage, time available and anything the curriculum needs to fit around.

The scope, subject coverage, delivery format and timing are agreed for each project. The tennis math map is a fictional planning sample; a complete course includes the teaching and materials that put a plan into practice.
