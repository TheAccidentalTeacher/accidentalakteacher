START WITH
THE PERSON.

Interests, ambitions, strengths, and sticking points belong in the design brief. I work from those realities toward a coherent sequence, useful practice, and a way to see what the learner can actually do. The format follows the learning: a course map, a field investigation, a printable, an interactive tool, or several working together.
