Explore my workTalk about a project

The learner is the starting point.

Interests, ambitions, strengths, and sticking points belong in the design brief. I work from those realities toward a coherent sequence, useful practice, and a way to see what the learner can actually do. The format follows the learning: a course map, a field investigation, a printable, an interactive tool, or several working together.

Curriculum and course design

Build the route from a learner's interests and goals to a purposeful sequence of knowledge, practice, and application.

Materials that teach

  • Workbooks and teaching guides
  • Worked examples and answer keys

  • Repeatable print-production pipelines
