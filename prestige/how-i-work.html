03Design and production

HOW THE
WORK GETS MADE.

I start with the person and the purpose. Then I make the educational choices visible enough to inspect, test, and improve.

01

Learn who it is for

Find out what the learner wants to do, what holds their attention, what they already know, and where the existing approach is failing them. Understand the family's or organization's constraints as well.

What comes out of this stepA clear learner and project brief.

02

Give the learning a destination

Choose what the learner should know and be able to do. Identify the evidence that would show it, then work backward toward the necessary knowledge and practice.

What comes out of this stepGoals, evidence, and the boundaries of the work.

03

Design a coherent sequence

Put the prerequisites in place. Connect new ideas to something meaningful. Decide where explanation, practice, discussion, investigation, and application belong.

What comes out of this stepA course map or unit sequence with a reason for its order.

04

Make the learning experience

Build the lessons, examples, activities, materials, and tools the plan needs. I direct AI-assisted production through specifications and source material, then inspect the output.

What comes out of this stepUsable materials or a working learning tool.

05

Check content and behavior

Check the facts, instructions, examples, and answers. For software, test the actual interaction. For print, inspect the rendered pages. A completed file is the start of the check.

What comes out of this stepReviewed content and evidence of the checks performed.

06

Revise from what happens

Use the learner's work, questions, and experience to identify the next change. Keep the learning goal in view while adjusting the route, pace, or format.

What comes out of this stepA recorded revision and a clear next step.

04Principles

One size fits one

A common goal can have more than one useful route. Personalization changes the route with a reason.

The learner does the thinking

Tools can support the work. The learner still needs opportunities to explain, apply, choose, and demonstrate understanding.

Make the decisions inspectable

Show the goals, sources, sequence, and evidence. That gives a parent, teacher, reviewer, or collaborator something concrete to examine.
