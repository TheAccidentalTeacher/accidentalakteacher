03Lesson design and print production

Wren and Sitka's Field Notebook

Twenty five two-page nature investigations for grades 3 to 5, designed around a teaching page, an activity, and a reason to notice the world outdoors.

Project type

Published learning materials

Status

Public notebook and TPT editions

The deliverables

  • 25 two-page nature investigations
  • Teaching, activity, and outdoor prompts
  • Grown-up notes and a complete answer key
  • 17 TPT editions: eight singles, eight pairs, and a bundle

Inspect the work

Explore the field notebook

01 / The need

Teach the idea. Let the child use it. Take it outdoors.

A science worksheet needs more than a question and an empty space. The learner needs enough teaching to begin, something useful to do with the idea, and support that a grown-up can actually use.

02 / Design decisions

  1. Give each investigation a paired teaching and activity structure. The information needed for the task belongs in the lesson itself.
  2. Finish each investigation with an outdoor observation, connecting the page to a cloud, track, leaf, shadow, or another accessible part of the real world.
  3. Include grown-up support and an answer key, and organize the work as eight single investigations, eight paired packets, and the complete notebook.

03 / What you can inspect

The public notebook page exposes the 25-investigation sequence and links the 17 TPT editions. Review the page to see the topic progression, teaching approach, and available formats.
