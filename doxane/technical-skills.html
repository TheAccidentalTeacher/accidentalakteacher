Technical skills

The tools behind the materials.

The stack matters because it determines what I can make useful: an interactive reference, a worksheet generator, a course website, or a repeatable production process.

My role

I design the learning experience, write the specification, and direct AI-assisted implementation. I inspect the output, test it against the brief, troubleshoot problems, and revise. The educational decisions and the responsibility for checking the work remain mine.

Checking the output

Files need a closer look.

Content and answer checks

Check factual claims, worked examples, instructions, answer keys, and the relationship between a task and its learning goal.

Rendered-page review

Inspect the actual printed or on-screen result for readability, page fit, hierarchy, missing assets, and broken references.

Behavior and access

Test the important interactions, responsive layouts, keyboard access, links, and state behavior in the experience people will use.

Controlled revisions

Keep source and output versions, record what changed, and verify the delivered version rather than relying on a successful build message.
