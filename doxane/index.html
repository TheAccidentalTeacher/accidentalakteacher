What I can create

A plan is the beginning.

What happens next depends on the learner and the task.

01

Curriculum and course design

Build the route from a learner's interests and goals to a purposeful sequence of knowledge, practice, and application.

  • Personalized curriculum maps
  • Full-course and unit planning
  • Learning goals and assessment plans
02

Materials that teach

Create something the learner can use, with the explanation, task, and support designed together.

  • Lessons and investigations
  • Workbooks and teaching guides
  • Worked examples and answer keys
03

Educational tools that work

  • Interactive learning activities
  • Searchable reference tools
  • Repeatable print-production pipelines
