A curriculum built around your child

Tell me what your child wants to do, what they need to learn, and where the current plan is getting in the way. I can build a course that gives those interests a serious place in the learning.

Begin with the learner

A useful brief has a person in it.

What does your child love doing? What are they working toward? What do they already understand, and where does the current approach lose them?

We also need the practical details: the subject, the time available, the support at home or school, and what the finished work needs to include.

“The learner is the starting point.”

Interests, ambitions, strengths, and sticking points belong in the design brief. I work from those realities toward a coherent sequence, useful practice, and a way to see what the learner can actually do. The format follows the learning: a course map, a field investigation, a printable, an interactive tool, or several working together.
