The starting point

The educational need.

The sample brief begins with a capable young tennis player who has come to associate math with repetitive worksheets. The design challenge is to retain a purposeful mathematical sequence while making the reason for the work visible to him.

The design work

Choices with a reason.

  1. 01

    Use the learner's tennis ambition as a continuing thread through the year, with four quarter themes rather than a collection of unrelated sports problems.

  2. 02

    Connect the mathematics to a statistics sheet, season budget, geometric reasoning, and a scouting report so the learner has something specific to produce.

  3. 03

    Keep prerequisites, assessment evidence, edge cases, and possible changes in the plan visible. An interest provides a route into the mathematics; the mathematics still has to be taught.

What was made

The deliverables.

  • A 36-week seventh-grade curriculum map
  • Four connected quarter themes
  • Application projects and evidence of learning
  • Pacing considerations and possible pivots
