Selected work/Wren and Sitka's Field Notebook

Lesson design and print production

A science lesson that ends outside.

Twenty five two-page nature investigations for grades 3 to 5, designed around a teaching page, an activity, and a reason to notice the world outdoors.

The starting point

The educational need.

A science worksheet needs more than a question and an empty space. The learner needs enough teaching to begin, something useful to do with the idea, and support that a grown-up can actually use.

The design work

Choices with a reason.

  1. 01

    Give each investigation a paired teaching and activity structure. The information needed for the task belongs in the lesson itself.

  2. 02

    Finish each investigation with an outdoor observation, connecting the page to a cloud, track, leaf, shadow, or another accessible part of the real world.

  3. 03

    Include grown-up support and an answer key, and organize the work as eight single investigations, eight paired packets, and the complete notebook.

What was made

The deliverables.

  • 25 two-page nature investigations
  • Teaching, activity, and outdoor prompts
  • Grown-up notes and a complete answer key
  • 17 TPT editions: eight singles, eight pairs, and a bundle
